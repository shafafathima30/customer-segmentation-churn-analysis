# Customer Segmentation & Churn Risk Analysis

## Project Overview

Analyzed real-world retail transaction data using Python and Pandas to understand customer purchasing behavior and identify customer segments based on their value and engagement.

## Business Questions

- Who are the most valuable customers?
- Which customers purchase frequently?
- Which customers have not purchased recently?
- Which customers may be at risk of becoming inactive?
- How can customer segments support retention strategies?

## Analysis Performed

- Cleaned transaction data by removing cancellations, missing customer IDs, and duplicate records.
- Calculated customer revenue using Quantity × UnitPrice.
- Built an RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis.
- Segmented customers into VIP, Loyal, Regular, and At Risk groups.
- Identified high-value customers within the At Risk segment.
- Compared customer segments by size, revenue, and average recency.
- Developed business recommendations for customer retention.

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Findings

- 47.68% of customers were classified as At Risk.
- VIP customers generated the highest total revenue.
- At Risk customers had the highest average recency.
- High-value At Risk customers represent an opportunity for targeted re-engagement campaigns.

## Dataset

The analysis uses the UCI Online Retail transactional dataset containing real-world retail purchase records.
